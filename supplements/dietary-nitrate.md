# Dietary Nitrate

## Overview

Dietary nitrate, often from beetroot juice concentrates, can improve exercise economy and endurance performance by increasing nitric oxide bioavailability via the nitrate–nitrite–NO pathway. It is one of the better-supported ergogenic aids for endurance and some high-intensity efforts.

## What It Is

Inorganic nitrate (NO3−) from vegetables or standardized beetroot products is reduced in the body to nitrite and nitric oxide, influencing blood flow and mitochondrial/muscle efficiency.

## Benefits

- Can improve endurance performance and time-trial outcomes in many studies
- May reduce oxygen cost of submaximal exercise
- Potential benefits for some high-intensity intermittent protocols
- Less established as a direct maximal-hypertrophy aid

## Who Should Take It

Endurance athletes and trainees seeking performance in prolonged or high-intensity aerobic efforts. Antibacterial mouthwash can blunt effects by disrupting oral bacteria needed for nitrate reduction.

## Recommended Dose

- Roughly 5–9 mmol nitrate (~300–600 mg), commonly provided by standardized beetroot concentrates
- Chronic multi-day loading plus acute pre-event dosing appears in many protocols
- Use products with disclosed nitrate content

## Timing

- Acute dose often ~2–3 hours before exercise
- Several days of elevated intake may enhance effects for some events
- Avoid antiseptic mouthwash around dosing windows when seeking ergogenic effects

## Side Effects

- Beeturia (red/pink urine or stool) is harmless
- GI upset possible with large juice volumes
- Blood-pressure lowering effect means caution with related clinical contexts

## Interactions

- Effects may be reduced by antibacterial mouthwash
- People on blood-pressure medications should seek clinical advice
- Distinct from citrulline, though both relate to NO pathways differently

## Evidence Summary

ISSN and multiple systematic reviews support nitrate/beetroot as an evidence-based ergogenic for endurance performance, with response variability (for example training status and event type). Evidence quality is high among sports supplements, though not universal across all athletes and events.
