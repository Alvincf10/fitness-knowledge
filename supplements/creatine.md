# Creatine

## Overview

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched sports supplements. It increases intramuscular phosphocreatine stores, supporting high-intensity efforts, strength gains, and lean mass accrual when combined with resistance training.

## What It Is

Creatine is a naturally occurring compound synthesized from amino acids and stored largely in skeletal muscle as phosphocreatine. Supplemental creatine monohydrate is the form with the strongest evidence base.

## Benefits

- Improves high-intensity performance and repeated-bout capacity
- Supports greater strength and lean mass gains with resistance training
- May aid recovery between hard efforts in anaerobic tasks
- Evidence extends across adolescents to older adults when used appropriately

## Who Should Take It

Strength and power athletes, recreational lifters seeking hypertrophy or performance, vegetarians/vegans with lower dietary creatine intake, and trainees who tolerate it well. Not required for health in people with adequate diets, but useful for performance goals.

## Recommended Dose

- Loading (optional): about 20 g/day split into 4 doses for 5–7 days
- Maintenance: 3–5 g/day
- Loading is unnecessary if consistent daily intake is preferred; muscle saturation simply takes longer

## Timing

Daily consistency matters more than precise peri-workout timing. Taking creatine with a meal is practical and may support uptake. Post-workout dosing is fine but not uniquely required.

## Side Effects

- Transient water retention / body-mass increase is common
- Gastrointestinal discomfort can occur with large single doses; split dosing helps
- Extensive research does not support kidney harm in healthy individuals at recommended doses

## Interactions

- Generally compatible with protein, caffeine, and beta-alanine
- People with known kidney disease or those on relevant medications should seek clinical advice before use
- Stay adequately hydrated as part of normal training practice

## Evidence Summary

ISSN and multiple meta-analyses rate creatine monohydrate as effective for high-intensity exercise, strength, and lean mass. Evidence grade is among the highest in sports nutrition. Other forms rarely outperform monohydrate on outcomes that matter.
