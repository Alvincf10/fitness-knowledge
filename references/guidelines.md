# Guidelines

Curated index of clinical and professional guidelines used as citation sources.

## Priority Sources

- ACSM guidelines and related consensus documents
- ISSN position stands and practical recommendations
- NSCA essentials and related professional guidance

## Indexed Guidelines

Guideline entries will be expanded during article generation. Record:

- Organization
- Title or short label
- Year
- Topic relevance (training, nutrition, supplements, recovery)

## Example Format

```
ACSM Position Stand (2022)
ISSN Position Stand
NSCA Essentials
```

## Notes

- Prefer organization-backed guidelines over commercial content
- Keep this index synchronized with recurring citations across articles
