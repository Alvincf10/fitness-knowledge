# Systematic Reviews

Curated index of systematic reviews and meta-analyses prioritized for citations in this knowledge base.

## Example Format

```
Schoenfeld BJ (2017)
Morton RW (2018)
```

## Indexed Reviews

Entries will be added as knowledge articles are authored. Prefer:

- Meta-analyses on training volume, frequency, and hypertrophy
- Systematic reviews on protein intake and body composition
- Reviews on creatine, caffeine, and other well-studied supplements
- Reviews on fat-loss interventions and recovery

## Notes

- Record author and year in simple APA form
- Prefer human data over animal-only evidence when available
- Update this file whenever a new high-quality review becomes a recurring citation
