# What is RPE in lifting?

## Question

What is RPE in lifting?

## Short Answer

Rate of Perceived Exertion estimates set difficulty; often paired with RIR.

## Detailed Explanation

RPE 10 ≈ no reps left; RPE 7–9 is common for working sets depending on the lift.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
