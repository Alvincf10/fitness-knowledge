# Is it okay if the Chest Dip feels harder on one side?

## Question

Is it okay if the Chest Dip feels harder on one side?

## Short Answer

Mild asymmetries are common; large persistent gaps deserve unilateral accessories and technique checks.

## Detailed Explanation

Address big side-to-side differences with single-arm/leg work and video review. Painful asymmetry needs professional assessment.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
