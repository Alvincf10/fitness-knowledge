# How many meals per day are optimal?

## Question

How many meals per day are optimal?

## Short Answer

There is no single optimal meal count; choose a pattern you can sustain that hits protein and calories.

## Detailed Explanation

Anywhere from about 2–6 meals can work. Bodybuilders often prefer 3–5 for protein distribution.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
