# How many times per week should I practice the deadlift for strength?

## Question

How many times per week should I practice the deadlift for strength?

## Short Answer

Often 1–3 times weekly depending on fatigue cost and experience.

## Detailed Explanation

Heavy technique practice benefits from frequency, but recovery limits how often you can go near maximal.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
