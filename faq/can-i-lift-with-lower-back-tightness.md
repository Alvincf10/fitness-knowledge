# Can I lift with lower back tightness?

## Question

Can I lift with lower back tightness?

## Short Answer

Mild tightness after training can be okay with regressions; radiating pain, numbness, or sharp pain needs professional evaluation.

## Detailed Explanation

Reduce load, swap to supported variations, and prioritize technique. This is not a diagnosis.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
