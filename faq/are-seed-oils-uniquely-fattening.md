# Are seed oils uniquely fattening?

## Question

Are seed oils uniquely fattening?

## Short Answer

No unique fattening effect beyond calories. Overall diet pattern matters more.

## Detailed Explanation

Focus on energy balance, protein, and food quality rather than single-oil fear narratives.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
