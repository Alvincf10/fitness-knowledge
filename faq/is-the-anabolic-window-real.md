# Is the Anabolic Window Real?

## Question

Do I need protein immediately after training or will I lose my gains?

## Short Answer

A narrow 30-minute “anabolic window” is overstated for most fed trainees. Daily protein intake and repeated feedings matter more than slamming a shake the second you rack the bar.

## Detailed Explanation

Muscle protein synthesis rises after training and protein feeding, but the practical window is measured in hours, especially if you ate protein before lifting. Immediate post-workout nutrition is still useful for convenience, recovery between close sessions, and hitting daily totals. Panic timing does not outperform a solid daily protein plan.

## Scientific Evidence

Nutrient-timing reviews (Aragon, Schoenfeld, ISSN) show total daily protein dominates hypertrophy outcomes versus minute-level urgency for typical gym trainees. Evidence grade A.

## Practical Advice

- Hit 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day protein first
- Eat a protein-rich meal within a few hours post-training
- Use a shake when whole food will be delayed
- Fuel harder if you trained fasted
