# Is Intermittent Fasting Better?

## Question

Is intermittent fasting better than a normal meal schedule for fat loss or muscle?

## Short Answer

Not inherently. When calories and protein are matched, intermittent fasting usually produces similar fat loss. Muscle outcomes depend on hitting protein and training fuel, which can be harder in short eating windows.

## Detailed Explanation

IF is an eating schedule, not a metabolic cheat code. Some people adhere better with fewer eating hours; others under-eat protein or train poorly while fasted. Choose the structure that helps you hit targets without impairing sleep, training, or relationship with food.

## Scientific Evidence

RCTs generally show similar weight loss for IF versus continuous restriction at matched calories. Evidence grade B for equivalence rather than superiority.

## Practical Advice

- Use IF only if it improves adherence
- Keep protein high inside the eating window
- Place workouts where you can fuel them
- Switch back to regular meals if strength or protein intake suffers
