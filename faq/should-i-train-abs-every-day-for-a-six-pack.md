# Should I train abs every day for a six-pack?

## Question

Should I train abs every day for a six-pack?

## Short Answer

No. Abs need progressive overload and low enough body fat to show; daily high-rep sit-ups are unnecessary.

## Detailed Explanation

Treat abs like other muscles with quality hard sets a few times weekly plus diet for visibility.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
