# Is cracking joints during lifting dangerous?

## Question

Is cracking joints during lifting dangerous?

## Short Answer

Painless cavitation noises are often benign; pain, swelling, or instability is not.

## Detailed Explanation

Do not force cracks. Assess technique and load if noise comes with discomfort.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
