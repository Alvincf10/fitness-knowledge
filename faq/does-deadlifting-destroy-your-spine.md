# Does deadlifting destroy your spine?

## Question

Does deadlifting destroy your spine?

## Short Answer

No. Deadlifts are a skill under load—risk rises with poor technique and excessive fatigue, not the pattern itself.

## Detailed Explanation

Neutral-ish bracing, progressive loading, and recovery management keep hinge work productive for most trainees.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
