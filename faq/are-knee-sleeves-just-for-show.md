# Are knee sleeves just for show?

## Question

Are knee sleeves just for show?

## Short Answer

They can provide warmth and subjective support; they do not fix poor programming or technique.

## Detailed Explanation

Use as comfort tools, not injury cures.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
