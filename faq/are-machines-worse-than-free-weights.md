# Are Machines Worse Than Free Weights?

## Question

Are machines inferior to free weights for building muscle?

## Short Answer

No. Machines can build muscle effectively when hard sets and progressive overload are applied. Free weights add balance and coordination demands; they are not uniquely required for hypertrophy.

## Detailed Explanation

Hypertrophy depends on tension, volume, and effort. Leg presses, pulldowns, and chest machines can overload target muscles with less skill demand and often easier progressive loading. Free weights excel for transferable strength skills and versatile patterns. Best programs often mix both: free-weight compounds plus machines for safe hard volume.

## Scientific Evidence

Comparative studies frequently show similar hypertrophy when effort and volume are matched across modalities. Evidence grade B.

## Practical Advice

- Use free weights for skillful compounds you want to master
- Use machines to add quality hard sets safely
- Progress load/reps on both
- Choose based on joints, preference, and available equipment
