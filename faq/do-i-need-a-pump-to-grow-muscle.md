# Do I need a pump to grow muscle?

## Question

Do I need a pump to grow muscle?

## Short Answer

No. A pump is optional; progressive tension matters more than transient swelling.

## Detailed Explanation

Pumps reflect fluid and metabolite accumulation. They can accompany good training but are not a reliable hypertrophy requirement.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
