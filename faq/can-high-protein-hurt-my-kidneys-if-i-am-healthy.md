# Can high protein hurt my kidneys if I am healthy?

## Question

Can high protein hurt my kidneys if I am healthy?

## Short Answer

Evidence does not show kidney harm from higher protein intakes in healthy people with normal kidney function.

## Detailed Explanation

People with kidney disease need medical supervision. Healthy lifters commonly use 1.6–2.2 g/kg safely in research contexts.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
