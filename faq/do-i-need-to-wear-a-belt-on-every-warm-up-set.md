# Do I need to wear a belt on every warm-up set?

## Question

Do I need to wear a belt on every warm-up set?

## Short Answer

Usually no as a blanket rule—use techniques that preserve technique and recovery.

## Detailed Explanation

About whether to wear a belt on every warm-up set: strength thrives on high-quality heavy practice, not maximal fatigue or risky habits every session.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
