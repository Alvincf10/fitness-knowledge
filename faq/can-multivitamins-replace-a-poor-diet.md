# Can multivitamins replace a poor diet?

## Question

Can multivitamins replace a poor diet?

## Short Answer

No. They may cover small gaps but cannot replace protein, fiber, and overall food quality.

## Detailed Explanation

Build the diet first; use a multi only as optional insurance.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
