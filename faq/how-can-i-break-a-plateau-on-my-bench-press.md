# How can I break a plateau on my bench press?

## Question

How can I break a plateau on my bench press?

## Short Answer

Audit technique, sleep, and recovery; then use a small overload strategy (microloads, paused reps, or volume adjustments).

## Detailed Explanation

Plateaus are normal. Deload if fatigued, then return with structured progression rather than random max attempts.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
