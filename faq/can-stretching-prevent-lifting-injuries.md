# Can stretching prevent lifting injuries?

## Question

Can stretching prevent lifting injuries?

## Short Answer

Static stretching alone is a weak injury-prevention strategy compared with gradual load progression and warm-ups.

## Detailed Explanation

Use specific warm-ups and sensible programming. Mobility work helps when it improves your positions.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
