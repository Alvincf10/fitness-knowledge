# Does massage build muscle?

## Question

Does massage build muscle?

## Short Answer

No. Massage may help relaxation or soreness perception but does not replace training tension.

## Detailed Explanation

Use it as optional recovery care, not a hypertrophy method.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
