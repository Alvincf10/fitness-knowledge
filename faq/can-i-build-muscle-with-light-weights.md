# Can I build muscle with light weights?

## Question

Can I build muscle with light weights?

## Short Answer

Yes, if you take sets close to failure and progress over time.

## Detailed Explanation

Light-load training near failure can hypertrophy muscle. Moderate loads are often easier to progress and recover from.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
