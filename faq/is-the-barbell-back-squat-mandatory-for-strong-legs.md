# Is the barbell back squat mandatory for strong legs?

## Question

Is the barbell back squat mandatory for strong legs?

## Short Answer

No. Any progressive lower-body compound you can load safely can build strong legs.

## Detailed Explanation

Back squats are excellent, but leg press, hack squat, and split-squat variations are valid alternatives.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
