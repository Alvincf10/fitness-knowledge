# Can I train shoulders every day?

## Question

Can I train shoulders every day?

## Short Answer

Daily low-volume shoulders work can work for some advanced approaches, but most people do better with 2–4 focused sessions weekly.

## Detailed Explanation

High frequency requires careful per-session volume. Recovery of local tissues still matters.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
