# Does muscle turn to fat if I stop lifting?

## Question

Does muscle turn to fat if I stop lifting?

## Short Answer

No. Muscle and fat are different tissues; stopping training can reduce muscle while fat rises if calories are high.

## Detailed Explanation

Detraining reduces muscle size over time. Fat gain requires energy surplus. They can happen together but one does not convert into the other.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
