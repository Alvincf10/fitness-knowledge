# Can isometric holds improve strength?

## Question

Can isometric holds improve strength?

## Short Answer

Yes, especially near sticking points, as a supplement to dynamic lifting.

## Detailed Explanation

Isometrics raise force at specific joint angles. Pair them with full-ROM dynamic training for best transfer.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
