# Can I train two days in a row?

## Question

Can I train two days in a row?

## Short Answer

Yes if muscle-group overlap and fatigue are managed—upper/lower splits do this often.

## Detailed Explanation

Train hard while recovered enough to keep technique. Daily max effort on the same lifts is usually poor.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
