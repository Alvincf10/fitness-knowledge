# Is intermittent fasting bad for muscle gain?

## Question

Is intermittent fasting bad for muscle gain?

## Short Answer

Not inherently, but it can make hitting protein and training energy harder for some lifters.

## Detailed Explanation

If you can train well and hit protein within your feeding window, muscle gain remains possible.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
