# Should I max out every Monday?

## Question

Should I max out every Monday?

## Short Answer

Usually no as a blanket rule—use techniques that preserve technique and recovery.

## Detailed Explanation

About whether to max out every Monday: strength thrives on high-quality heavy practice, not maximal fatigue or risky habits every session.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
