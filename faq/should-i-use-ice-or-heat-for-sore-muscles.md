# Should I use ice or heat for sore muscles?

## Question

Should I use ice or heat for sore muscles?

## Short Answer

For routine DOMS, gentle movement and time usually suffice; ice/heat preferences vary and are not magic healers.

## Detailed Explanation

Persistent swelling or acute injuries warrant professional guidance rather than internet protocols.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
