# Do I need carbs at night after training?

## Question

Do I need carbs at night after training?

## Short Answer

No special requirement. Place carbs where they best support performance and adherence.

## Detailed Explanation

Post-workout carbs can replenish glycogen and are convenient, but daily totals dominate.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
