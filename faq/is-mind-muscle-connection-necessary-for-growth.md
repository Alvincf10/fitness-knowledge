# Is mind-muscle connection necessary for growth?

## Question

Is mind-muscle connection necessary for growth?

## Short Answer

Helpful for some isolation work, but not mandatory if load, ROM, and effort are solid.

## Detailed Explanation

Internal focus can raise activation on isolation lifts for some people. Heavy compounds usually benefit more from stable external cues and progressive overload.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
