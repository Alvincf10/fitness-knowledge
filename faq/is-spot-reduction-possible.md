# Is Spot Reduction Possible?

## Question

Can I burn fat from my stomach or thighs by doing endless abs or thigh exercises?

## Short Answer

Not in any meaningful targeted way. Fat loss is systemic and driven by a sustained calorie deficit. Local exercises build local muscle; they do not selectively melt fat from that area.

## Detailed Explanation

Spot reduction myths persist because people want control over stubborn fat regions. Resistance training can grow abdominal or glute muscles, improving appearance once body fat falls. Cardio and ab circuits burn calories but do not override whole-body fat distribution genetics. Reduce total fat with diet and training, then let regional fat loss follow individual patterns.

## Scientific Evidence

Classic and modern studies generally fail to show practical spot reduction from local exercise alone. Energy-balance research explains fat loss as whole-body. Evidence grade A against meaningful spot reduction.

## Practical Advice

- Create a moderate calorie deficit
- Keep lifting to retain muscle
- Train abs/glutes for strength and shape, not as fat burners
- Be patient with stubborn regions
