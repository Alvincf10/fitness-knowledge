# Where should I feel the Dumbbell Row working?

## Question

Where should I feel the Dumbbell Row working?

## Short Answer

Primarily in the target muscle group for that pattern, with secondary muscles assisting—not sharp joint pain.

## Detailed Explanation

If you only feel joints, regress load/ROM and review setup. Sensation is a cue, not perfect proof of stimulus.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
