# Do I need to track calories forever?

## Question

Do I need to track calories forever?

## Short Answer

No. Tracking is a temporary teaching tool for many people, then habits can take over.

## Detailed Explanation

Use tracking to learn portions. Transition to mindful portions if adherence is better that way.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
