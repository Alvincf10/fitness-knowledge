# Are rest days wasted days?

## Question

Are rest days wasted days?

## Short Answer

No. Adaptation happens during recovery between hard sessions.

## Detailed Explanation

Planned rest supports long-term progress and injury-risk reduction.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
