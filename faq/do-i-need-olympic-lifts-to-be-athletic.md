# Do I need Olympic lifts to be athletic?

## Question

Do I need Olympic lifts to be athletic?

## Short Answer

No. They are excellent tools but not mandatory for general fitness or hypertrophy.

## Detailed Explanation

Sprints, jumps, and basic strength lifts can build athleticism with lower technical demand.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
