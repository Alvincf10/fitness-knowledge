# Do I need protein immediately after training?

## Question

Do I need protein immediately after training?

## Short Answer

No urgent magical window for most lifters; eat protein within a few hours around training.

## Detailed Explanation

Total daily protein and overall diet quality dominate. Post-workout meals are convenient, not mandatory within minutes.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
