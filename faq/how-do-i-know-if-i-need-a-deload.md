# How do I know if I need a deload?

## Question

How do I know if I need a deload?

## Short Answer

Persistent performance drops, nagging aches, poor sleep, and motivation crashes despite food/sleep efforts are clues.

## Detailed Explanation

Do not wait for burnout. Scheduled deloads prevent reactive ones.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
