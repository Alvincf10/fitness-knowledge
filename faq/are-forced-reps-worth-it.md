# Are forced reps worth it?

## Question

Are forced reps worth it?

## Short Answer

Occasionally as an advanced intensity technique, not as a default for every set.

## Detailed Explanation

Forced reps raise fatigue. Most progress comes from planned progressive sets you control yourself.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
