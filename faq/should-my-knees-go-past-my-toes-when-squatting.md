# Should my knees go past my toes when squatting?

## Question

Should my knees go past my toes when squatting?

## Short Answer

They can—blocking that cue is outdated for many lifters. Knees should track well over feet.

## Detailed Explanation

Anthropometry dictates shin angle. Avoid painful cave-ins rather than fearing forward knees.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
