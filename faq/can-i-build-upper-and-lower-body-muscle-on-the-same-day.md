# Can I build upper and lower body muscle on the same day?

## Question

Can I build upper and lower body muscle on the same day?

## Short Answer

Yes. Full-body sessions are effective when recovery and session length stay manageable.

## Detailed Explanation

Full-body, upper/lower, and body-part splits can all work. Choose based on schedule and recovery.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
