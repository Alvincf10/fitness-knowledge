# How Long Should I Rest Between Sets?

## Question

How long should I rest between sets for muscle and strength?

## Short Answer

Rest long enough to perform high-quality reps: often about 1–2 minutes for many isolation/hypertrophy sets and 2–5 minutes for heavy compounds. Cutting rest too short can reduce load and volume quality.

## Detailed Explanation

Shorter rests raise metabolic stress but may reduce reps and load on subsequent sets. Research comparing rest intervals often finds longer rests produce equal or better hypertrophy when they preserve volume-load. Strength training clearly benefits from longer recovery between heavy sets. Auto-regulate: if bar speed and form collapse, rest longer.

## Scientific Evidence

Rest-interval research and ACSM-style recommendations support longer rests for strength and sufficient rests to maintain volume for hypertrophy. Evidence grade B.

## Practical Advice

- Heavy squats/benches/rows: start around 2–3+ minutes
- Isolation work: often 60–120 seconds
- Prioritize set quality over rushing
- Keep rest consistent when tracking progression
