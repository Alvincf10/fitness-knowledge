# Is fish oil required if I eat fatty fish?

## Question

Is fish oil required if I eat fatty fish?

## Short Answer

Not required if intake of EPA/DHA from fish is already adequate.

## Detailed Explanation

Supplements help people who rarely eat fish. Dose based on need, not marketing.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
