# Do I need free weights to build muscle?

## Question

Do I need free weights to build muscle?

## Short Answer

No. Machines, cables, and bodyweight can all build muscle if overloaded.

## Detailed Explanation

Free weights are versatile, but hypertrophy depends on tension and progression, not equipment brand.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
