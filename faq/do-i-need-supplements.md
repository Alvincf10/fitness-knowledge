# Do I Need Supplements?

## Question

Do I need supplements to see results from training?

## Short Answer

No. Training, protein, calories, sleep, and consistency produce nearly all results. A few supplements (notably creatine, and protein powder for convenience) can help, but none are required.

## Detailed Explanation

Supplements are additive, not foundational. Creatine has strong performance evidence. Whey helps only if it raises total protein. Caffeine can acutely improve performance. Most fat burners and “test boosters” lack comparable support. Fix food and programming before building a large supplement stack.

## Scientific Evidence

ISSN rankings consistently place creatine, caffeine, and protein among the best-supported options, while many marketed products have weak evidence. Evidence grade A that supplements are optional.

## Practical Advice

- Master progressive training and daily protein first
- Consider creatine monohydrate as the first optional add-on
- Use protein powder only for convenience
- Ignore stacks that promise dramatic fat loss without a deficit
