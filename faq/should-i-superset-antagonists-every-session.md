# Should I superset antagonists every session?

## Question

Should I superset antagonists every session?

## Short Answer

It depends on context, but progressive hard sets and recoverable weekly volume matter more than any single tactic.

## Detailed Explanation

Regarding whether to superset antagonists every session, treat it as an optional strategy. Keep progressive overload, protein intake, and recovery intact. If the tactic helps adherence or stimulus without wrecking recovery, it can stay; otherwise drop it.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
