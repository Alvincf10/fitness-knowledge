# Are fat burners worth it?

## Question

Are fat burners worth it?

## Short Answer

Most over-the-counter fat burners offer small or inconsistent benefits compared with diet adherence.

## Detailed Explanation

Caffeine can help performance and appetite for some people. No supplement replaces a deficit.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
