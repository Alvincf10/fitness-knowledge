# Should I cut dairy to lose fat?

## Question

Should I cut dairy to lose fat?

## Short Answer

Only if you prefer it or have intolerance—dairy is not uniquely fattening.

## Detailed Explanation

Dairy foods can be protein-rich and satiating inside a deficit.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
