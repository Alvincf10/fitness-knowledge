# How Much Cardio for Fat Loss?

## Question

How much cardio do I need to lose fat?

## Short Answer

None is strictly required if your calorie deficit comes from diet alone—but moderate cardio can help increase expenditure, improve health, and preserve adherence. More is not better if it wrecks lifting recovery.

## Detailed Explanation

Fat loss requires a calorie deficit. Cardio is one tool to create or enlarge that deficit. Walking and low-impact work are often easiest to recover from. High-volume intense cardio can compete with lower-body strength sessions. Many lifters do well with daily steps plus 1–3 optional cardio sessions while keeping protein and weights high.

## Scientific Evidence

Energy-balance and ACSM guidance show exercise helps weight management, with diet usually driving most of the deficit. Evidence grade A for deficit requirement; cardio amount is individual.

## Practical Advice

- Set protein and a moderate deficit first
- Add steps before aggressive HIIT stacks
- Keep progressive resistance training
- Increase cardio gradually if fat loss stalls and recovery allows
