# When should I see a professional about training pain?

## Question

When should I see a professional about training pain?

## Short Answer

Seek care for sharp, radiating, night, traumatic, or persistent pain that limits function beyond a few days of smart regression.

## Detailed Explanation

Early assessment beats months of guesswork. Coaches should not diagnose medical conditions.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
