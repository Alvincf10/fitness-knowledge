# Is linear progression best for beginners?

## Question

Is linear progression best for beginners?

## Short Answer

Yes for many novices—add small loads regularly while technique holds.

## Detailed Explanation

Beginners adapt quickly. When session-to-session jumps stall, switch to weekly progression.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
