# Should I Do Cardio Before or After Weights?

## Question

If I do cardio and weights the same day, which should come first?

## Short Answer

For muscle and strength goals, lift first when both are hard. Do easy steps either before as a warm-up or after. Save hard intervals for after lifting or for separate sessions.

## Detailed Explanation

Fatigue from intense cardio can reduce squat and press performance if done first. Resistance training adaptations depend on lifting quality. Low-intensity cardio has less interference. Ideal separation is different sessions or different times of day when schedule allows.

## Scientific Evidence

Concurrent-training research suggests prioritizing the adaptation you care about most within a session. Evidence grade B for sequencing practical guidelines.

## Practical Advice

- Hypertrophy/strength priority: weights before hard cardio
- Use 5–10 minutes easy cardio as warm-up if desired
- Keep post-lift cardio moderate when legs were trained hard
- Separate sessions if both need to be high quality
