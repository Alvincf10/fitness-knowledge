# What is a realistic fat-loss rate for women?

## Question

What is a realistic fat-loss rate for women?

## Short Answer

Often about 0.25–1.0% of body weight per week depending on starting size and deficit aggressiveness.

## Detailed Explanation

Slower rates usually preserve training quality and muscle better. Adjust using weekly averages, not daily scale noise.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
