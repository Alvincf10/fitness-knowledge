# Should I train through sharp joint pain?

## Question

Should I train through sharp joint pain?

## Short Answer

No. Sharp or worsening joint pain is a stop signal—regress the movement and seek professional care if it persists.

## Detailed Explanation

Training with mild transient discomfort differs from sharp pain. Deload, change ROM/variation, and refer out when needed.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
