# Are machines bad for building strength?

## Question

Are machines bad for building strength?

## Short Answer

No. Machines build strength on their movement pattern; free-weight strength is more specific to free weights.

## Detailed Explanation

Use machines for overload and joint-friendly volume. Include free-weight patterns if those skills matter to you.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
