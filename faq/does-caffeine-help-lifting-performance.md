# Does caffeine help lifting performance?

## Question

Does caffeine help lifting performance?

## Short Answer

Yes for many people—improved alertness and performance at moderate doses.

## Detailed Explanation

Typical effective doses vary; too much impairs sleep and anxiety. Time it away from bedtime.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
