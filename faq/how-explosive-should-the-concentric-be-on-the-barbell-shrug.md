# How explosive should the concentric be on the Barbell Shrug?

## Question

How explosive should the concentric be on the Barbell Shrug?

## Short Answer

Move the concentric with intent while keeping control—especially as loads get heavy.

## Detailed Explanation

Soft eccentrics and lazy concentrics leave stimulus on the table. Intent to move fast with control is a useful default.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
