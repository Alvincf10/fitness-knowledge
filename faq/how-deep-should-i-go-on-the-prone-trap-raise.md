# How deep should I go on the Prone Trap Raise?

## Question

How deep should I go on the Prone Trap Raise?

## Short Answer

Use the deepest controllable range that keeps joints comfortable and positions stacked—unless a shorter ROM is programmed on purpose.

## Detailed Explanation

Depth should preserve neutral-ish joint stacking and tension on the target muscles. Painful end ranges are not trophies. Film from the side and progress ROM gradually.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
