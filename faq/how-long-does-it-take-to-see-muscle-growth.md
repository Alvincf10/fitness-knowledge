# How long does it take to see muscle growth?

## Question

How long does it take to see muscle growth?

## Short Answer

Beginners may notice changes within weeks; clearer hypertrophy often takes 8–12+ consistent weeks.

## Detailed Explanation

Strength rises quickly from neural adaptations. Visible size changes depend on genetics, body fat, protein, sleep, and program quality.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
