# Are low-carb diets better for losing fat?

## Question

Are low-carb diets better for losing fat?

## Short Answer

Not inherently. Matched calories and protein usually produce similar fat loss across carb levels.

## Detailed Explanation

Choose carbs based on training performance and adherence, not mythology.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
