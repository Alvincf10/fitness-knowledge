# What is more important for strength: technique or ego loading?

## Question

What is more important for strength: technique or ego loading?

## Short Answer

Technique. Loads that destroy positions do not build transferable strength reliably.

## Detailed Explanation

Clean reps with progressive overload beat sloppy maximal attempts that spike injury risk.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
