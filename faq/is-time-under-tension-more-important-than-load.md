# Is time under tension more important than load?

## Question

Is time under tension more important than load?

## Short Answer

Neither alone wins. Hard sets with controlled tempo and progressive tension matter most.

## Detailed Explanation

Extremely slow tempos with tiny loads are usually inferior to challenging loads taken near failure through a solid ROM.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
