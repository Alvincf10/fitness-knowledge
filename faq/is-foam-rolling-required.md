# Is foam rolling required?

## Question

Is foam rolling required?

## Short Answer

No. It can feel good as a warm-up adjunct but is not mandatory for gains.

## Detailed Explanation

Spend more time on progressive training and sleep than on endless rolling.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
