# Is late-night protein useless?

## Question

Is late-night protein useless?

## Short Answer

No. Protein consumed later still counts toward daily totals and can support overnight aminoacidemia.

## Detailed Explanation

Casein or mixed meals at night are fine if they fit calories and sleep.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
