# How Fast Can I Lose Fat?

## Question

How quickly can I lose fat without wrecking muscle and adherence?

## Short Answer

A sustainable rate for many lifters is roughly 0.25–1.0% of body weight per week, with slower rates better when already lean. Faster losses raise muscle-loss and rebound risk.

## Detailed Explanation

Faster deficits create quicker scale drops but often sacrifice training quality, hormones, and adherence. Water and glycogen shifts can masquerade as fat loss in week one. Prioritize protein, lifting, sleep, and a deficit you can sustain for weeks. Use diet breaks if fatigue accumulates.

## Scientific Evidence

Physique-athlete and obesity-management literature favor moderate rates for lean-mass retention and adherence. Evidence grade B for specific percentage ranges as practical heuristics.

## Practical Advice

- Start with a moderate deficit and weekly average weigh-ins
- Keep protein high and keep lifting hard
- Slow down when already lean
- Expect non-linear weekly scale changes
