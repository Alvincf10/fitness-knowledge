# Should beginners do high-volume bodybuilding programs?

## Question

Should beginners do high-volume bodybuilding programs?

## Short Answer

Usually no. Start with moderate volume and progress as recovery allows.

## Detailed Explanation

Beginners grow on lower volumes. Excess volume increases fatigue before skill and recovery capacity catch up.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
