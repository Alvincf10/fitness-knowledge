# Is soreness the same as an injury?

## Question

Is soreness the same as an injury?

## Short Answer

No. DOMS is delayed muscle soreness; injury often involves sharper, localized, or function-limiting pain.

## Detailed Explanation

DOMS usually peaks 24–72 hours after novel stress. Injury patterns differ—get assessed if unsure.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
