# How often should I test a true 1RM?

## Question

How often should I test a true 1RM?

## Short Answer

Infrequently for most lifters—often every 8–12+ weeks—or estimate from heavy triples/fives.

## Detailed Explanation

Frequent maxing increases fatigue and risk. Planned peaking is better than weekly all-out singles for non-competitors.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
