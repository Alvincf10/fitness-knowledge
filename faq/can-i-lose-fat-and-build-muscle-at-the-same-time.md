# Can I lose fat and build muscle at the same time?

## Question

Can I lose fat and build muscle at the same time?

## Short Answer

Yes for beginners, returners, and people with higher body fat; harder for lean advanced lifters.

## Detailed Explanation

Recomposition needs high protein, hard progressive training, and a small deficit or maintenance.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
