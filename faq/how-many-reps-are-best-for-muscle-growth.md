# How many reps are best for muscle growth?

## Question

How many reps are best for muscle growth?

## Short Answer

Most hypertrophy work works well in roughly 6–20 reps per set when sets are hard and weekly volume is sufficient.

## Detailed Explanation

Hypertrophy depends mainly on mechanical tension from hard sets over time. A wide rep range can grow muscle if proximity to failure is similar. Moderate reps are usually the most practical for accumulating quality volume.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
