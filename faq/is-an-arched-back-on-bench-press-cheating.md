# Is an arched back on bench press cheating?

## Question

Is an arched back on bench press cheating?

## Short Answer

A moderate upper-back arch is normal bench setup; excessive bridging that lifts glutes may be contest-illegal or risky.

## Detailed Explanation

Retract scapulae and keep glutes down for general training unless powerlifting rules allow controlled arching.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
