# Is creatine safe long term?

## Question

Is creatine safe long term?

## Short Answer

Creatine monohydrate has a strong safety record in healthy people at standard doses.

## Detailed Explanation

ISSN and long-term studies support safety for typical 3–5 g/day use. Those with kidney disease should seek medical advice.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
