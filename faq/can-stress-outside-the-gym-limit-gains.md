# Can stress outside the gym limit gains?

## Question

Can stress outside the gym limit gains?

## Short Answer

Yes. High life stress can reduce recovery, sleep quality, and training performance.

## Detailed Explanation

Manage load when stress spikes—deload volume before ego loading.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
