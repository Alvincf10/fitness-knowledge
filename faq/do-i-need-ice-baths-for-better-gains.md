# Do I need ice baths for better gains?

## Question

Do I need ice baths for better gains?

## Short Answer

Not for hypertrophy. Frequent cold exposure right after lifting may blunt some growth signaling.

## Detailed Explanation

Reserve aggressive cold for sport recovery contexts, not as a default bodybuilding tool.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
