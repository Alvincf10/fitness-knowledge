# Do I need cardio to lose fat?

## Question

Do I need cardio to lose fat?

## Short Answer

No. A calorie deficit drives fat loss; cardio is optional support for expenditure and health.

## Detailed Explanation

Nutrition creates most of the deficit. Lifting protects muscle. Cardio helps if it fits recovery and preference.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
