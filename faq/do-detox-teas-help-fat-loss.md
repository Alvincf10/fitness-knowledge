# Do detox teas help fat loss?

## Question

Do detox teas help fat loss?

## Short Answer

No meaningful fat-loss mechanism beyond possible water loss or calorie displacement.

## Detailed Explanation

Sustainable deficit and protein intake beat detox marketing.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
