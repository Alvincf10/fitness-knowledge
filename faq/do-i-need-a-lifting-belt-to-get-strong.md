# Do I need a lifting belt to get strong?

## Question

Do I need a lifting belt to get strong?

## Short Answer

No. Belts can help heavy braced lifts but are optional.

## Detailed Explanation

A belt increases intra-abdominal pressure with a proper brace. Learn bracing with and without a belt.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
