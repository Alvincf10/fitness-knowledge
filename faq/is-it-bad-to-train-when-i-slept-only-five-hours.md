# Is it bad to train when I slept only five hours?

## Question

Is it bad to train when I slept only five hours?

## Short Answer

You can do a lighter technical session, but repeatedly training hard on poor sleep impairs progress and safety.

## Detailed Explanation

Autoregulate down when sleep is poor. Fix sleep before adding volume.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
