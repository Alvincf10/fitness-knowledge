# Should I train explosively for strength?

## Question

Should I train explosively for strength?

## Short Answer

Intent to move heavy loads fast is useful; turning every set into a speed session is unnecessary.

## Detailed Explanation

Concentric intent improves rate of force development. Still keep controlled eccentrics and solid technique.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
