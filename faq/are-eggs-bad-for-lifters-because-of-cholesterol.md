# Are eggs bad for lifters because of cholesterol?

## Question

Are eggs bad for lifters because of cholesterol?

## Short Answer

For most healthy people, eggs can fit a training diet; individual medical advice may differ.

## Detailed Explanation

Dietary cholesterol responses vary. Eggs are protein-dense. People with specific lipid disorders should follow clinician guidance.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
