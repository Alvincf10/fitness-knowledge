# Does ashwagandha build muscle directly?

## Question

Does ashwagandha build muscle directly?

## Short Answer

Evidence is mixed/limited compared with creatine; any benefit is likely indirect via stress/sleep for some users.

## Detailed Explanation

It is not a primary hypertrophy supplement. Prioritize training, protein, creatine, and sleep.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
