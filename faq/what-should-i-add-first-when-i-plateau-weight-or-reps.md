# What should I add first when I plateau: weight or reps?

## Question

What should I add first when I plateau: weight or reps?

## Short Answer

Usually add reps within your target range, then increase load when the top of the range is clean.

## Detailed Explanation

Double progression is simple and effective for hypertrophy and general strength.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
