# How should I distribute protein across the day?

## Question

How should I distribute protein across the day?

## Short Answer

Aim for several feedings (often 3–5) that each provide a meaningful protein dose when practical.

## Detailed Explanation

Daily total matters most. Distribution helps maximize muscle protein synthesis opportunities.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
