# Should I prioritize isolation or compound lifts for hypertrophy?

## Question

Should I prioritize isolation or compound lifts for hypertrophy?

## Short Answer

Use compounds as the base, then add isolation to finish volume for specific muscles.

## Detailed Explanation

Compounds efficiently load multiple muscles. Isolation helps lagging muscles without as much systemic fatigue.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
