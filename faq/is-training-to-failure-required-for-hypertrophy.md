# Is training to failure required for hypertrophy?

## Question

Is training to failure required for hypertrophy?

## Short Answer

No. Most sets taken near failure (about 0–3 RIR) are enough; constant failure adds fatigue.

## Detailed Explanation

When volume and effort are closely matched, failure and non-failure training often produce similar growth. Use failure selectively on safer isolation movements.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
