# How Often Should I Train?

## Question

How many days per week should I lift for muscle growth?

## Short Answer

Most people do well lifting 3–5 days per week, training each major muscle about twice weekly. Total hard weekly sets and recovery matter more than any single split.

## Detailed Explanation

Frequency mainly distributes weekly volume. Hitting a muscle twice per week often improves set quality versus cramming all sets into one marathon session. Beginners can grow on full-body three days weekly. Intermediates often use upper/lower or push/pull/legs. Training every day can work only if volume per session and recovery are managed; it is not required.

## Scientific Evidence

Frequency meta-analyses show similar hypertrophy when weekly volume is equated, with practical advantages for 2+ exposures per muscle. Evidence grade A.

## Practical Advice

- Start with 3–4 lifting days you can sustain
- Aim to train each major muscle ~2 times weekly
- Split high weekly volumes across sessions
- Add a day only if recovery and progression remain solid
