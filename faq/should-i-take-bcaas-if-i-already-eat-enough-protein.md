# Should I take BCAAs if I already eat enough protein?

## Question

Should I take BCAAs if I already eat enough protein?

## Short Answer

Usually no. Whole protein already provides BCAAs.

## Detailed Explanation

BCAAs are redundant for most people hitting protein targets from food or whey.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
