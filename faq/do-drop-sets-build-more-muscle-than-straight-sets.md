# Do drop sets build more muscle than straight sets?

## Question

Do drop sets build more muscle than straight sets?

## Short Answer

Not clearly more when total hard volume is matched; they can save time.

## Detailed Explanation

Drop sets raise density and fatigue. Use them sparingly for isolation or time-efficient finishers.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
