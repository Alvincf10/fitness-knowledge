# Should I deadlift every week for strength?

## Question

Should I deadlift every week for strength?

## Short Answer

Not necessarily. Many lifters pull heavy weekly or biweekly depending on recovery.

## Detailed Explanation

Deadlifts are systemically fatiguing. Use frequency that preserves technique and progress on related hinges/rows.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
