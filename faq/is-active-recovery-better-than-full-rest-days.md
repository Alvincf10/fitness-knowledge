# Is active recovery better than full rest days?

## Question

Is active recovery better than full rest days?

## Short Answer

Both work. Light activity can help soreness perception; complete rest is fine too.

## Detailed Explanation

Choose based on fatigue and preference. Easy walks are a low-risk default.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
