# Does alcohol hurt muscle growth?

## Question

Does alcohol hurt muscle growth?

## Short Answer

Heavy intake impairs recovery and muscle protein synthesis; light infrequent intake is less damaging but not helpful.

## Detailed Explanation

Keep alcohol low around hard training blocks and cuts.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
