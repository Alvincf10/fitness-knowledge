# Should I grind every single at all costs?

## Question

Should I grind every single at all costs?

## Short Answer

Usually no as a blanket rule—use techniques that preserve technique and recovery.

## Detailed Explanation

About whether to grind every single at all costs: strength thrives on high-quality heavy practice, not maximal fatigue or risky habits every session.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
