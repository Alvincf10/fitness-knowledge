# What is double progression?

## Question

What is double progression?

## Short Answer

A method where you increase reps within a range, then increase load and restart the rep range.

## Detailed Explanation

Example: 3×8–12—when you hit 12 on all sets cleanly, add weight and build back up.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
