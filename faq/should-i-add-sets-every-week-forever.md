# Should I add sets every week forever?

## Question

Should I add sets every week forever?

## Short Answer

No. Raise volume in phases, then deload or reset when recovery lags.

## Detailed Explanation

Volume has a recoverable upper limit. Progress load/reps first when possible.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
