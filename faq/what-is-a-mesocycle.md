# What is a mesocycle?

## Question

What is a mesocycle?

## Short Answer

A medium training block, often several weeks, with a theme such as volume accumulation or strength emphasis.

## Detailed Explanation

Mesocycles help organize progressive overload and planned deloads.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
