# Is full range of motion always better for hypertrophy?

## Question

Is full range of motion always better for hypertrophy?

## Short Answer

Full controllable ROM is usually preferred, but partials can be useful tools.

## Detailed Explanation

Longer ROMs often increase stimulus per rep for many lifts. Lengthened partials or pinned partials can supplement, not always replace, full ROM.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
