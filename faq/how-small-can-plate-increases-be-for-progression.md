# How small can plate increases be for progression?

## Question

How small can plate increases be for progression?

## Short Answer

As small as your equipment allows—microplates help upper-body lifts especially.

## Detailed Explanation

Jumping 5 kg per side forever stalls. Microloading keeps overload smooth.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
