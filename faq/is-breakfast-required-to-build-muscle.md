# Is breakfast required to build muscle?

## Question

Is breakfast required to build muscle?

## Short Answer

No. Total daily intake matters more than eating immediately upon waking.

## Detailed Explanation

Breakfast can help some people hit protein targets. Others do well with later first meals.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
