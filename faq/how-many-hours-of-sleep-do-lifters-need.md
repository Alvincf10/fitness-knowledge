# How many hours of sleep do lifters need?

## Question

How many hours of sleep do lifters need?

## Short Answer

Most adults do best near 7–9 hours; hard training often feels better closer to the higher end.

## Detailed Explanation

Sleep restriction impairs recovery, hormones, and effort. Prioritize consistency.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
