# How many times per week should I train each muscle for growth?

## Question

How many times per week should I train each muscle for growth?

## Short Answer

About 2+ hard sessions per muscle per week is a strong default for most intermediates.

## Detailed Explanation

Frequency helps distribute weekly volume. Total hard sets and recovery matter more than any single split.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
