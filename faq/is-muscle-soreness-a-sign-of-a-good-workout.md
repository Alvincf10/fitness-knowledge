# Is muscle soreness a sign of a good workout?

## Question

Is muscle soreness a sign of a good workout?

## Short Answer

No. Soreness is a weak guide to hypertrophy quality.

## Detailed Explanation

DOMS often follows novelty or eccentric stress. Productive progressive training can produce little soreness.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
