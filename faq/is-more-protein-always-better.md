# Is More Protein Always Better?

## Question

If 2 g/kg is good, is 4 g/kg better for muscle growth?

## Short Answer

No. Benefits plateau. Once you are around roughly 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day with solid training, extra protein mainly displaces other macros or adds calories.

## Detailed Explanation

Protein has a clear dose–response up to an optimal range for hypertrophy. Beyond that, additional intake does little for muscle gain in healthy lifters. Very high intakes can still be used for satiety during cuts, but they are not required for growth. Training stimulus and energy availability remain decisive.

## Scientific Evidence

Protein supplementation meta-analyses show diminishing returns above evidence-based ranges. Evidence grade A.

## Practical Advice

- Anchor intake near 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day
- Use higher intakes mainly for hunger control while cutting
- Do not neglect carbs needed for hard training
- Focus next upgrades on progressive overload and sleep
