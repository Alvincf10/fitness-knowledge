# How Many Sets Per Week?

## Question

How many sets per muscle group per week should I do to grow?

## Short Answer

A practical starting range for many intermediates is about 10–20 hard sets per muscle per week, adjusted for recovery and progress. Quality near failure matters more than counting easy warm-ups.

## Detailed Explanation

Hypertrophy shows a dose–response to weekly hard sets, with diminishing returns at very high volumes. Beginners often grow on the low end. Advanced lifters may need more for stubborn muscles, but only if set quality stays high. Spread volume across 2+ sessions when totals climb. Track performance and soreness to titrate.

## Scientific Evidence

Volume meta-analyses (Schoenfeld and related reviews) support higher weekly sets producing more growth up to recoverable limits. Evidence grade A.

## Practical Advice

- Count working sets near 0–3 RIR
- Start near ~10–12 weekly sets for a muscle, then add if stalled
- Reduce volume during poor sleep or high life stress
- Prioritize compounds, then add isolation for lagging areas
