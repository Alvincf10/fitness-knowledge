# What is a deload week?

## Question

What is a deload week?

## Short Answer

A planned reduction in training stress to improve recovery while keeping some practice.

## Detailed Explanation

Common approaches cut volume substantially and/or reduce load for 5–7 days after hard blocks.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
