# Does sweating more mean more fat loss?

## Question

Does sweating more mean more fat loss?

## Short Answer

No. Sweat is water and cooling, not fat.

## Detailed Explanation

Hot gyms and extra clothing increase water loss, not fat oxidation.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
