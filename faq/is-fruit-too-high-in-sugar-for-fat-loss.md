# Is fruit too high in sugar for fat loss?

## Question

Is fruit too high in sugar for fat loss?

## Short Answer

No. Whole fruit is generally compatible with fat loss inside a calorie deficit.

## Detailed Explanation

Fruit provides fiber and micronutrients. Juice is easier to overconsume than whole fruit.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
