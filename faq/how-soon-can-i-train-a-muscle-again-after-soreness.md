# How soon can I train a muscle again after soreness?

## Question

How soon can I train a muscle again after soreness?

## Short Answer

You can often train with mild soreness if technique and force output remain solid; severe soreness warrants easier work.

## Detailed Explanation

Soreness alone is not a perfect readiness test. Use performance and joint feel.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
