# Do I need to change exercises every week to grow?

## Question

Do I need to change exercises every week to grow?

## Short Answer

No. Keep main lifts stable long enough to progress; rotate accessories as needed.

## Detailed Explanation

Constant exercise switching interferes with progressive overload tracking. Variation is useful, not mandatory weekly.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
