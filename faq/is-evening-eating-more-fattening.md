# Is evening eating more fattening?

## Question

Is evening eating more fattening?

## Short Answer

No. Total daily energy balance matters more than clock time for most people.

## Detailed Explanation

Meal timing can affect adherence and sleep; it does not uniquely store evening calories as fat.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
