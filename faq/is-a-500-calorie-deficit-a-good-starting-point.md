# Is a 500 calorie deficit a good starting point?

## Question

Is a 500 calorie deficit a good starting point?

## Short Answer

Often yes as a moderate starting estimate, then adjust from weekly averages.

## Detailed Explanation

Larger deficits are faster but harder to sustain and riskier for muscle loss.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
