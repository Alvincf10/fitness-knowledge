# Is creatine just water weight?

## Question

Is creatine just water weight?

## Short Answer

Early gains include water in muscle, but creatine also supports training quality and lean mass over time.

## Detailed Explanation

Intramuscular water is expected. Performance and training volume improvements can drive further adaptations.
