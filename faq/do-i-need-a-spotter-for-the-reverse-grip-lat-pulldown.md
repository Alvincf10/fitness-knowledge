# Do I need a spotter for the Reverse-Grip Lat Pulldown?

## Question

Do I need a spotter for the Reverse-Grip Lat Pulldown?

## Short Answer

For heavy free-weight presses and risky setups, a spotter or safety pins are strongly recommended.

## Detailed Explanation

Safeties and responsible loading beat bravado. Machines reduce but do not eliminate the need for attention.

## Evidence

NSCA technique standards and biomechanics principles; individual anthropometry changes optimal form details. Evidence grade: B.
