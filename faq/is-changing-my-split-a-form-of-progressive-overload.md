# Is changing my split a form of progressive overload?

## Question

Is changing my split a form of progressive overload?

## Short Answer

Not by itself. Overload is increased stimulus; split changes only redistribute work.

## Detailed Explanation

A new split can help logistics, but you still need harder sets over time.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
