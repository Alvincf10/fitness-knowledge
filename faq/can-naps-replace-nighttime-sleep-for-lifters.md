# Can naps replace nighttime sleep for lifters?

## Question

Can naps replace nighttime sleep for lifters?

## Short Answer

Naps help but rarely fully replace inadequate night sleep.

## Detailed Explanation

Use short naps for debt; prioritize a consistent night schedule for hormonal and cognitive recovery.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
