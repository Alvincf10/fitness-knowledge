# Does blood flow restriction training work?

## Question

Does blood flow restriction training work?

## Short Answer

Yes as an adjunct for hypertrophy with light loads, especially when heavy loading is limited.

## Detailed Explanation

BFR can enhance light-load growth stimuli. Use proper cuffs/education and avoid if contraindicated medically.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
