# Do squats ruin your knees?

## Question

Do squats ruin your knees?

## Short Answer

No. Well-programmed squats are generally knee-friendly; poor load management or pre-existing issues matter more.

## Detailed Explanation

Evidence does not support the myth that squatting below parallel inherently destroys healthy knees.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
