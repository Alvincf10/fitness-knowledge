# Are BCAAs Worth It?

## Question

Should I buy BCAA supplements for muscle growth?

## Short Answer

Usually no—if your daily protein intake from complete sources is already adequate. BCAAs alone are inferior to complete protein for hypertrophy.

## Detailed Explanation

Leucine helps trigger muscle protein synthesis, but growth still needs a full essential amino acid profile. Whey, meat, eggs, dairy, soy, or mixed meals already provide BCAAs. Isolated BCAAs make more sense only in rare low-protein situations, and even then an EAA or complete protein product is typically better.

## Scientific Evidence

Reviews (including Wolfe and ISSN-aligned protein literature) argue BCAA supplements are unnecessary for protein-sufficient trainees. Evidence grade C for meaningful hypertrophy benefit in typical lifters.

## Practical Advice

- Spend money on total protein food or whey first
- Skip BCAAs if you already hit protein targets
- Use whole protein peri-workout instead of BCAA sips
- Reassess only if clinical or dietary constraints limit complete protein
