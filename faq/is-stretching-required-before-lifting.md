# Is Stretching Required Before Lifting?

## Question

Do I have to static stretch before lifting weights?

## Short Answer

No. A general warm-up plus light movement-specific sets is usually enough. Long static stretching right before maximal strength work is not required and can temporarily reduce power if excessive.

## Detailed Explanation

Warm-ups raise temperature and rehearse technique. Dynamic mobility and ramp-up sets prepare you for squats, presses, and pulls. Static stretching can still help long-term flexibility when done after training or in separate sessions. Pre-lift priorities are comfort through the needed range and crisp first working sets—not a long passive stretch routine.

## Scientific Evidence

Warm-up and stretching reviews support activity-specific preparation; prolonged pre-event static stretching may impair acute strength/power. Evidence grade B.

## Practical Advice

- Do 5–10 minutes of easy movement
- Use 1–3 ramp-up sets on main lifts
- Stretch after lifting if mobility is a goal
- Address painful limitations with appropriate professional care when needed
