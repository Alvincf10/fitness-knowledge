# What rep range is best for maximal strength?

## Question

What rep range is best for maximal strength?

## Short Answer

Heavy work in roughly 1–6 reps is most specific, supported by accessory hypertrophy work.

## Detailed Explanation

Strength is highly specific to load and task. Heavy practice improves neural efficiency and 1RM skill.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
