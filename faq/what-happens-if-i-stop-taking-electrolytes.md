# What happens if I stop taking electrolytes?

## Question

What happens if I stop taking electrolytes?

## Short Answer

Any benefits tied to elevated levels of electrolytes gradually return toward baseline; you do not lose all training progress overnight.

## Detailed Explanation

Skill, muscle, and habits remain. Supplement effects are supportive, not the main driver of long-term adaptations.

## Evidence

ISSN sports-supplement position stands and systematic reviews for the specific ingredient when available. Evidence grade: B.
