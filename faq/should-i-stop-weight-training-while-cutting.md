# Should I stop weight training while cutting?

## Question

Should I stop weight training while cutting?

## Short Answer

No. Keep lifting to preserve muscle and metabolic health.

## Detailed Explanation

You may reduce volume if fatigued, but maintain hard sets on key lifts when possible.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
