# Should I eat back exercise calories when cutting?

## Question

Should I eat back exercise calories when cutting?

## Short Answer

Usually not fully—trackers often overestimate burn. Eat for the planned deficit.

## Detailed Explanation

If energy crashes, add food strategically rather than mirroring noisy wearable estimates.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
