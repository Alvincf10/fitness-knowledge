# What should I do if my hips hurt during lifting?

## Question

What should I do if my hips hurt during lifting?

## Short Answer

Stop the painful variation, regress load/ROM, and switch to a pain-tolerable pattern. Seek professional care if pain persists or worsens.

## Detailed Explanation

Joint pain is information. Temporary technique tweaks help some cases, but lasting hips pain needs assessment—this is not a diagnosis.

## Evidence

Sports-medicine load-management and tendinopathy principles; referral recommended for persistent pain. Not diagnosis. Evidence grade: B.
