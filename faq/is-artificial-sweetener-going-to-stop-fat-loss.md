# Is artificial sweetener going to stop fat loss?

## Question

Is artificial sweetener going to stop fat loss?

## Short Answer

Unlikely for most people when total calories remain in deficit.

## Detailed Explanation

Use them if they help adherence; monitor individual appetite responses.

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
