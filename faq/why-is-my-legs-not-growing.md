# Why is my legs not growing?

## Question

Why is my legs not growing?

## Short Answer

Usually insufficient hard weekly volume, poor progression, low protein/calories, or inconsistent technique on legs movements.

## Detailed Explanation

Log sets near failure for 6–8 weeks, raise volume modestly, and verify ROM. Nutrition and sleep must support growth.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
