# Will lifting weights slow fat loss?

## Question

Will lifting weights slow fat loss?

## Short Answer

No. Lifting usually improves composition during a cut by helping retain muscle.

## Detailed Explanation

Scale weight can fluctuate with glycogen and water. Track trends, waist, and strength.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
