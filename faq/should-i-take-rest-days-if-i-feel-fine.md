# Should I take rest days if I feel fine?

## Question

Should I take rest days if I feel fine?

## Short Answer

Planned rest still helps long-term progress even when motivation is high.

## Detailed Explanation

Feeling good is not the same as being recovered. Schedule rest or easy days proactively.

## Evidence

Sleep and recovery reviews; overreaching literature; ACSM recovery considerations for trainees. Evidence grade: A.
