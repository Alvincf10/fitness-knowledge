# Do I Need Creatine?

## Question

Do I need creatine to build muscle or get stronger?

## Short Answer

No, you do not need it—but creatine monohydrate is one of the most effective, well-researched optional supplements for strength, power, and lean mass when training is progressive.

## Detailed Explanation

Creatine increases phosphocreatine stores and supports high-intensity performance. It is not a steroid and not required for progress if training, protein, sleep, and progressive overload are in place. It is a high-value add-on for many lifters because benefits are reliable and cost is low. Vegetarians may see larger relative responses due to lower dietary creatine.

## Scientific Evidence

ISSN position stands and multiple meta-analyses support creatine monohydrate for high-intensity exercise and lean-mass gains with resistance training. Evidence grade A.

## Practical Advice

- Use 3–5 g creatine monohydrate daily if you want the benefit
- Loading is optional
- Keep training progressive; creatine cannot replace effort
- People with kidney disease should seek clinical advice before use
