# Does strength training make you bulky?

## Question

Does strength training make you bulky?

## Short Answer

No. Strength training alone does not automatically create large visible bulk without surplus calories and years of growth.

## Detailed Explanation

Women and many men gain strength faster than visible size. Bulk requires hypertrophy-focused volume plus energy availability.

## Evidence

ACSM progression models; NSCA strength literature; load-specificity research for maximal strength. Evidence grade: A.
