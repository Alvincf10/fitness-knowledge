# Can I grow muscle without getting sore?

## Question

Can I grow muscle without getting sore?

## Short Answer

Yes. Lack of soreness does not mean lack of stimulus.

## Detailed Explanation

As you adapt, the same workload causes less DOMS. Track progressive overload instead of soreness.

## Evidence

Schoenfeld and related hypertrophy reviews; ACSM resistance-training position stands; NSCA programming guidance. Evidence grade: A.
