# Can I progress with only bodyweight exercises?

## Question

Can I progress with only bodyweight exercises?

## Short Answer

Yes—via harder variations, slower tempos, pauses, elevated feet, and added load.

## Detailed Explanation

Push-up to deficit/weighted/ring progressions are classic examples.

## Evidence

ACSM progressive-overload principles; periodization and autoregulation (RIR/RPE) literature. Evidence grade: A.
