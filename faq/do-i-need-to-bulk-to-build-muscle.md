# Do I Need to Bulk to Build Muscle?

## Question

Must I eat in a calorie surplus to build muscle?

## Short Answer

A surplus often maximizes the rate of muscle gain, especially for intermediates and advanced lifters, but beginners and others can gain muscle near maintenance or even in a mild deficit.

## Detailed Explanation

Energy availability influences hypertrophy rate. Surpluses support recovery and growth when training is progressive. They are not magic and should stay controlled to limit excess fat. If you prefer slower recomposition, you can delay a bulk—especially as a novice. Advanced lean athletes usually need a surplus for meaningful further growth.

## Scientific Evidence

Reviews on energy surplus and physique development support faster gains with controlled surplus in many trained populations, with exceptions for novices/recomposition contexts. Evidence grade B.

## Practical Advice

- Novices: prioritize training and protein; surplus optional at first
- Intermediates wanting faster size: use a modest surplus
- Avoid dreamer bulks
- Use maintenance between phases
