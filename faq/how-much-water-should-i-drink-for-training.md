# How much water should I drink for training?

## Question

How much water should I drink for training?

## Short Answer

Drink enough to keep urine pale-yellow and replace fluids around hard/sweaty sessions; exact liters vary by person.

## Detailed Explanation

Thirst, environment, and sweat rate matter. Electrolytes help in long sweaty sessions.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
