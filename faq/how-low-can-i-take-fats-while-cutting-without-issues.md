# How low can I take fats while cutting without issues?

## Question

How low can I take fats while cutting without issues?

## Short Answer

Keep dietary fat from going extremely low; many lifters stay near ~0.3–0.6+ g/kg depending on preference and hormones/health context.

## Detailed Explanation

Very low fat can hurt adherence, hormones, and vitamin absorption. Individual medical contexts differ.

## Evidence

ISSN and ACSM nutrition position stands; protein and energy-balance meta-analyses where applicable. Evidence grade: A.
