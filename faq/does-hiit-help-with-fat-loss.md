# Does HIIT help with fat loss?

## Question

Does HIIT help with fat loss?

## Short Answer

Yes as part of a sustainable expenditure strategy, provided diet creates a deficit.

## Detailed Explanation

HIIT can help conditional fitness and calorie burn but is easy to overdo beside hard lifting. Combine with protein intake and lifting for better composition outcomes.

## Evidence

Energy-balance principles; ACSM/ISSN guidance on deficits, protein, and resistance training during weight loss. Evidence grade: A.
