# Seated Cable Row

## Overview

The seated cable row is a horizontal pulling exercise that loads the latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, trapezius, and elbow flexors under continuous cable tension. It is a foundational machine/cable row for back hypertrophy and pulling strength.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Latissimus dorsi
- Rhomboids and middle trapezius

### Secondary

- Rear deltoids
- Biceps and other elbow flexors
- Erector spinae (isometric depending on torso angle)

### Stabilizers

- Core musculature
- Scapular stabilizers
- Hamstrings/hips if feet are braced

## Equipment

- Seated cable row station
- V-handle, straight bar, or rope
- Foot platform

## Difficulty

beginner

## Execution

1. Sit tall with feet braced; hold the handle with arms extended and shoulders packed.
2. Pull the handle toward the torso by driving elbows back.
3. Retract the scapulae at the end range without shrugging into the ears.
4. Return under control to a stretch position without rounding excessively through the lumbar spine.
5. Keep the torso angle relatively stable (avoid large rock).

## Coaching Cues

- "Pull elbows back"
- "Chest tall"
- "Squeeze the shoulder blades"
- "Control the reach"

## Common Mistakes

- Excessive torso rocking
- Shrugging traps
- Rounding the low back under load
- Cutting the stretch
- Using only arm curl motion

## Breathing

Inhale to prepare or during the eccentric lengthening phase. Exhale during the concentric pull (or near peak contraction). Brace the trunk; avoid breath-holding that causes dizziness on high-rep sets.

## Range of Motion

Arms long with a loaded stretch to elbows-back peak contraction near the torso.

## Progressions

- Heavier load
- Pauses at contraction
- Single-arm cable row
- Chest-supported free-weight rows

## Regressions

- Chest-supported machine row
- Lighter load with stricter torso
- Inverted row

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–4 sets

### Reps

- 8–15

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3

### Rest

- 60–120 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–3x weekly

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Primary horizontal-pull hypertrophy staple.

#### Strength

Builds general pulling strength.

#### Endurance

Higher-rep sets work well.

#### Fat Loss

Efficient back compound.

## Contraindications

May be inappropriate with symptomatic shoulder impingement, acute elbow tendinopathy, recent lat or biceps strain, uncontrolled low-back pain during hinged positions, or inability to control scapular motion. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
