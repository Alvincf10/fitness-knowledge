# Dumbbell Row

## Overview

The dumbbell row is a unilateral bent-over or supported horizontal pull that loads the latissimus dorsi and upper back through a large ROM, while allowing natural shoulder pathing and side-to-side balancing.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Latissimus dorsi
- Rhomboids and middle trapezius

### Secondary

- Rear deltoids
- Elbow flexors
- Spinal erectors (depending on support)

### Stabilizers

- Core anti-rotation
- Glute/hip of the stance leg
- Scapular stabilizers

## Equipment

- Dumbbell
- Flat bench for hand/knee support (common)
- Optional: free staggered stance without bench

## Difficulty

beginner

## Execution

1. Place one hand and ipsilateral knee on a bench (or hinge with free hand braced).
2. Hold the dumbbell in the opposite hand, arm hanging long, spine neutral.
3. Row the elbow toward the hip/back pocket.
4. Lower to a deep stretch without rotating the pelvis excessively.
5. Complete reps, then switch sides.

## Coaching Cues

- "Elbow to hip"
- "Neutral spine"
- "Reach long at the bottom"
- "Do not yank with the torso"

## Common Mistakes

- Rotating and heaving the torso
- Shrugging the dumbbell
- Short ROM
- Rounding the low back

## Breathing

Inhale to prepare or during the eccentric lengthening phase. Exhale during the concentric pull (or near peak contraction). Brace the trunk; avoid breath-holding that causes dizziness on high-rep sets.

## Range of Motion

Full stretch hang to elbow-back contraction near the torso.

## Progressions

- Heavier dumbbells
- Kroc-style higher-rep heavy sets
- Pauses
- Meadows row

## Regressions

- Chest-supported dumbbell row
- Cable single-arm row
- Machine row

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–4 sets per side

### Reps

- Hypertrophy: 8–15
- Strength-biased: 6–10

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3

### Rest

- 60–120 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–3x weekly

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Cornerstone unilateral back hypertrophy exercise.

#### Strength

Builds unilateral pulling strength.

#### Endurance

Higher-rep Kroc-style sets possible for advanced trainees.

#### Fat Loss

Time-efficient unilateral compound.

## Contraindications

May be inappropriate with symptomatic shoulder impingement, acute elbow tendinopathy, recent lat or biceps strain, uncontrolled low-back pain during hinged positions, or inability to control scapular motion. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
