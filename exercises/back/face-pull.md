# Face Pull

## Overview

The face pull is a cable or band exercise pulling toward the face with external rotation bias, targeting the rear deltoids, rhomboids, and external rotators. It is widely used for upper-back balance and shoulder-health accessory work alongside heavier vertical and horizontal pulls.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Rear deltoids
- Rhomboids and middle/lower trapezius

### Secondary

- Infraspinatus and teres minor (external rotation component)
- Biceps (minor)

### Stabilizers

- Scapular stabilizers
- Core in standing variations

## Equipment

- Cable set at upper-chest/face height
- Rope attachment
- Optional band alternative

## Difficulty

beginner

## Execution

1. Set cable high; grip rope ends with thumbs toward yourself.
2. Step back into tension; start arms long.
3. Pull the rope toward the face/forehead while externally rotating so fists end near ears.
4. Squeeze rear delts/upper back; return under control.
5. Avoid shrugging into the neck or flaring into a pure upright row.

## Coaching Cues

- "Rope to face, knuckles back"
- "Elbows high but controlled"
- "Externally rotate at the finish"
- "No shrug"

## Common Mistakes

- Turning into an upright row with internal rotation
- Excessive load
- Lumbar lean cheating
- Ending with elbows low and no upper-back squeeze

## Breathing

Exhale on the pull; inhale on the return.

## Range of Motion

Arms long under tension to face-level finish with external rotation.

## Progressions

- Load within control
- Pauses at peak external rotation
- Single-arm face pull

## Regressions

- Band face pull
- Rear-delt fly light
- Prone Y/T raises

## Programming

### Sets

- 2–4 sets

### Reps

- 12–20

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3; technique priority

### Rest

- 45–75 seconds

### Frequency

- 2–4x weekly as accessory

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Upper-back/rear-delt accessory volume.

#### Strength

Not a primary strength lift.

#### Endurance

Excellent higher-rep option.

#### Fat Loss

Low-fatigue shoulder-balance work.

## Contraindications

Reduce load/ROM if shoulder pain appears. Not medical diagnosis.
