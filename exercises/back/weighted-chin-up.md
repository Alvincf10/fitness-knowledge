# Weighted Chin-Up

## Overview

The weighted chin-up adds external load to the supinated-grip chin-up, allowing continued overload of the lats and elbow flexors after bodyweight chin-ups no longer provide sufficient stimulus.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Latissimus dorsi
- Elbow flexors (high contribution)

### Secondary

- Rhomboids and middle trapezius
- Rear deltoids

### Stabilizers

- Core
- Scapular stabilizers
- Grip musculature

## Equipment

- Pull-up bar
- Weight belt, vest, or dumbbell

## Difficulty

advanced

## Execution

1. Secure load; hang with a supinated grip.
2. Pull strictly until chin clears the bar.
3. Lower to a controlled dead hang.
4. Maintain shoulder packing under load.
5. Dismount and unload safely.

## Coaching Cues

- "Pack, then pull"
- "Elbows down and back"
- "No kip"
- "Control the negative"

## Common Mistakes

- Excess load causing incomplete ROM
- Elbow irritation ignored
- Swinging
- Unsafe belt setup

## Breathing

Inhale to prepare or during the eccentric lengthening phase. Exhale during the concentric pull (or near peak contraction). Brace the trunk; avoid breath-holding that causes dizziness on high-rep sets.

## Range of Motion

Dead hang to chin-over-bar with control.

## Progressions

- Heavier load
- Pauses at the top
- Slow eccentrics

## Regressions

- Bodyweight chin-up
- Assisted chin-up
- Reverse-grip pulldown

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–5 sets

### Reps

- 3–10

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3

### Rest

- 2–5 minutes

### Frequency

- 1–2x weekly

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Strong lat + biceps vertical-pull overload.

#### Strength

High-value pulling strength variation.

#### Endurance

Prefer lighter variations.

#### Fat Loss

Optional if elbows tolerate loading.

## Contraindications

May be inappropriate with symptomatic shoulder impingement, acute elbow tendinopathy, recent lat or biceps strain, uncontrolled low-back pain during hinged positions, or inability to control scapular motion. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis. Monitor elbow flexor tendons closely.
