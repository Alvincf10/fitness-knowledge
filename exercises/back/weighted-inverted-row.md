# Weighted Inverted Row

## Overview

The weighted inverted row adds a vest or plate to the bodyweight horizontal pull once unweighted inverted rows no longer provide sufficient overload for hypertrophy or strength goals.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Latissimus dorsi
- Rhomboids and middle trapezius

### Secondary

- Rear deltoids
- Elbow flexors

### Stabilizers

- Core anti-extension
- Scapular stabilizers

## Equipment

- Bar or rings
- Weight vest or securely placed plate
- Spotter optional for plate loading

## Difficulty

intermediate

## Execution

1. Secure external load.
2. Perform full-ROM inverted rows with a rigid plank.
3. Pull chest to bar; lower to full extension.
4. Maintain alignment under load.
5. Remove load safely after the set.

## Coaching Cues

- "Load secure"
- "Same standards as bodyweight"
- "Chest to bar"
- "No sag"

## Common Mistakes

- Unsecured plates
- Cutting ROM under load
- Hip sag
- Excess load destroying positions

## Breathing

Inhale to prepare or during the eccentric lengthening phase. Exhale during the concentric pull (or near peak contraction). Brace the trunk; avoid breath-holding that causes dizziness on high-rep sets.

## Range of Motion

Full extension to chest-to-bar.

## Progressions

- Heavier vest
- Feet elevated + load
- Paused weighted rows

## Regressions

- Bodyweight inverted row
- Higher bar angle
- Cable row

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–4 sets

### Reps

- 5–12

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3

### Rest

- 90–150 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–3x weekly

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Keeps bodyweight horizontal pulls progressive.

#### Strength

Relative strength overload tool.

#### Endurance

Use lighter loads for higher reps.

#### Fat Loss

Effective minimal-equipment progression.

## Contraindications

May be inappropriate with symptomatic shoulder impingement, acute elbow tendinopathy, recent lat or biceps strain, uncontrolled low-back pain during hinged positions, or inability to control scapular motion. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
