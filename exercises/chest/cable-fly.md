# Cable Fly

## Overview

The cable fly (often a mid-height standing cable crossover/fly) isolates the pectoralis major through humeral adduction under continuous tension. Unlike dumbbell flies, cables maintain resistance near peak contraction, which is useful for hypertrophy accessories.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Pectoralis major

### Secondary

- Anterior deltoids
- Biceps brachii (minor stabilizing role)

### Stabilizers

- Core and scapular musculature in standing variations
- Rotator cuff for humeral control

## Equipment

- Dual cable station
- Handles
- Optional staggered stance for balance

## Difficulty

intermediate

## Execution

1. Set cables around mid-chest height. Grab handles and step forward into tension.
2. Start arms open with a slight elbow bend and soft scapular set.
3. Bring handles together in an arc via shoulder horizontal adduction.
4. Squeeze briefly at peak contraction without shrugging.
5. Return under control to a stretch without painful end-range positions.

## Coaching Cues

- "Soft elbows, fixed angle"
- "Hug a barrel"
- "Squeeze at the midline"
- "Control the stretch on the way out"

## Common Mistakes

- Turning the fly into a press
- Torso swing/momentum
- Overstretching into pain
- Shrugging traps
- Asymmetric heights or stance

## Breathing

Exhale as handles meet; inhale as arms open. Brace to avoid lumbar sway.

## Range of Motion

Open to a challenging comfortable stretch; close until hands meet or cross slightly based on shoulder comfort.

## Progressions

- Increase load
- Slow eccentrics
- Peak-contraction pauses
- Single-arm cable fly

## Regressions

- Pec deck
- Light dumbbell fly
- Supported machine fly

## Programming

### Sets

- 2–4 sets

### Reps

- 10–15 (hypertrophy default)

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3; prioritize tension over load

### Rest

- 60–90 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–3 times weekly as accessory volume

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Excellent isolation finisher with continuous tension.

#### Strength

Not a primary strength builder.

#### Endurance

Works well for higher-rep metabolic sets.

#### Fat Loss

Useful accessory after compound presses.

## Contraindications

Avoid deep stretch positions that provoke shoulder pain; reduce ROM or use supported machine fly. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
