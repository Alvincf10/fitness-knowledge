# Smith Machine Incline Press

## Overview

The Smith machine incline press combines an inclined torso with a rails-guided bar path for upper-chest biased pressing when free-weight spotting is limited.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Pectoralis major (clavicular emphasis)

### Secondary

- Anterior deltoids
- Triceps brachii

### Stabilizers

- Less than free barbell; scapular set still required

## Equipment

- Smith machine
- Incline bench aligned to upper-chest touch point
- Safety stops

## Difficulty

intermediate

## Execution

1. Set incline bench under the Smith bar path to upper chest.
2. Unhook, lower with control, press to extension.
3. Re-hook fully after the set.
4. Keep feet planted and upper back stable.
5. Avoid bouncing.

## Coaching Cues

- "Align bench before loading"
- "Safeties on"
- "Upper-chest touch"
- "Re-hook completely"

## Common Mistakes

- Misaligned bench
- Too-steep incline jammed against rails
- Ignoring safeties
- Excessive bounce

## Breathing

Inhale during the controlled eccentric (lowering). Brace the trunk, then exhale through the concentric press or near lockout. Avoid prolonged breath-holds on high-rep sets if dizziness occurs.

## Range of Motion

Touch upper chest if comfortable; shorten if rails force painful angles.

## Progressions

- Load
- Pauses
- Free-weight incline progressions

## Regressions

- Incline machine press
- Incline dumbbell press light
- Landmine press

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–4 sets

### Reps

- 6–12

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3

### Rest

- 90–180 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–2x weekly

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Guided upper-chest overload.

#### Strength

Limited transfer to free bar groove.

#### Endurance

Moderate.

#### Fat Loss

Optional compound.

## Contraindications

May be inappropriate during symptomatic anterior shoulder irritation, recent pectoralis or triceps strain, uncontrolled wrist/elbow pain, or when a safe setup/spot cannot be arranged. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
