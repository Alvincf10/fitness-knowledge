# Smith Machine Bench Press

## Overview

The Smith machine bench press is a rails-guided horizontal bar press. The fixed path reduces some balance demand and can allow confident loading, but it constrains natural bar path and may alter shoulder stress versus a free barbell. Treat it as a guided variation, not a perfect free-weight substitute.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Pectoralis major

### Secondary

- Triceps brachii
- Anterior deltoids

### Stabilizers

- Less whole-body stabilization than free barbell; scapular control still required

## Equipment

- Smith machine
- Flat bench aligned to mid-chest bar path
- Safety stops set appropriately

## Difficulty

intermediate

## Execution

1. Position the bench so the bar descends to mid-chest; set safeties.
2. Lie down, plant feet, and set the upper back.
3. Rotate to unhook; lower under control to the chest.
4. Press to extension without bouncing.
5. Re-hook securely before exiting.

## Coaching Cues

- "Bench placement first, then load"
- "Set safeties before heavy sets"
- "Touch with control"
- "Re-hook fully at the end"

## Common Mistakes

- Misaligned bench (bar path toward neck or belly)
- Ignoring safeties
- Excessive bouncing
- Forcing painful elbow angles on the fixed path
- Assuming the machine fixes technique errors

## Breathing

Inhale during the controlled eccentric (lowering). Brace the trunk, then exhale through the concentric press or near lockout. Avoid prolonged breath-holds on high-rep sets if dizziness occurs.

## Range of Motion

Touch the chest if comfortable. Shorten ROM or switch variations if the fixed path irritates the shoulders.

## Progressions

- Load increases
- Pause reps
- Tempo eccentrics
- Smith incline press

## Regressions

- Machine chest press
- Dumbbell bench press
- Push-up

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–4 sets

### Reps

- 6–12 typical

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3

### Rest

- 90–180 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–2 sessions weekly as press variety

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Useful guided overload tool for chest volume.

#### Strength

Can build pressing capacity; transfer to free barbell groove is imperfect.

#### Endurance

Moderate; other options may feel more natural at high reps.

#### Fat Loss

Acceptable compound when setup is quick and safe.

## Contraindications

Discontinue if the fixed path provokes sharp shoulder pain. Verify hooks/safeties. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
