# Chest Dip

## Overview

The chest dip is a parallel-bar press performed with a slight forward torso lean to bias the pectoralis major relative to a more upright triceps-dominant dip. It is a high-tension compound movement for intermediate+ trainees when shoulder comfort allows.

## Muscles Worked

### Primary

- Pectoralis major

### Secondary

- Triceps brachii
- Anterior deltoids

### Stabilizers

- Scapular depressors and retractors
- Core musculature
- Rotator cuff

## Equipment

- Parallel bars or dip station
- Optional assistance band or machine
- Optional weight belt for progression

## Difficulty

intermediate

## Execution

1. Support on parallel bars with elbows extended and shoulders depressed.
2. Lean slightly forward and descend by bending the elbows.
3. Lower until upper arms are near parallel or to a pain-free depth.
4. Press to full extension while maintaining the forward lean bias.
5. Keep the descent controlled; avoid bouncing.

## Coaching Cues

- "Slight lean for chest bias"
- "Shoulders packed down"
- "Chest between the bars"
- "Press the bars away"

## Common Mistakes

- Excessive depth causing anterior shoulder pain
- Too-upright torso when chest bias is intended
- Kipping/swinging
- Shrugging
- Uncontrolled flare under fatigue

## Breathing

Inhale on the descent; exhale on the press.

## Range of Motion

Parallel upper-arm depth is a common target; deeper only if controlled and pain-free.

## Progressions

- Weighted dips
- Slower eccentrics
- Ring dips (advanced)

## Regressions

- Band-assisted dips
- Machine-assisted dips
- Push-up variations

## Programming

### Sets

- 3–4 sets

### Reps

- Bodyweight hypertrophy: 6–12
- Weighted strength-biased: 4–8

### Intensity

- RIR 1–3; start conservative on depth

### Rest

- 90–180 seconds

### Frequency

- 1–2 times weekly for most lifters due to joint stress

### Best Goals

#### Hypertrophy

Excellent compound chest/triceps builder when tolerated.

#### Strength

Strong relative-strength and weighted progression tool.

#### Endurance

Higher reps possible with assistance.

#### Fat Loss

Keep if recovered; otherwise substitute machine press.

## Contraindications

Often poorly tolerated with anterior shoulder irritation, AC joint pain, or inadequate scapular control. Reduce depth or choose another press. This is training guidance, not medical diagnosis.
